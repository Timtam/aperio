---
title: "08 – Search"
---

In this chapter you quickly find events and tasks again – across every
connected calendar and list.

## Searching

1. Open search (default: `Ctrl+F`).
2. Type a search term – Aperio searches **titles**, **descriptions** and
   **locations**.
3. Results appear as a list as you type.
4. Use the arrow keys to select a result and `Enter` to open it.

## Narrowing results

You can filter the search, for example by:

- **type** (events only or tasks only),
- **calendar / list**,
- **time range**.

This lets you quickly find the right entry even among many.

## Tips

- Search includes **all visible sources**. If you hide a source in the
  sidebar, it is also left out of search.
- A selected result opens in its place in the calendar or list, so you see
  the context.
- Coming back from a result keeps your search: the term and the filters are
  still there, and the results are looked up again, so an entry you just
  edited comes back the way it is now. Closing search ends it – the next
  `Ctrl+F` starts on an empty field.

> **Screen-reader note:** The search field is linked to the result list
> (combobox pattern). The number of matches is announced, and you move
> through the results with the arrow keys without leaving the input field.
> `Esc` closes search.

## Summary

You can search across sources, filter results and open them directly. In the
next chapter we set up synchronization between devices.
