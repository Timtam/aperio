title: "Welcome to Aperio"
---

Aperio is a calendar and task planner built from the ground up to be **fully
usable with a screen reader** – without ever having to switch to browse
mode. At the same time, it is a fast, native desktop app. It also runs as a
**mobile app** on iOS and Android – see [Mobile app](/guides/mobile/).

This guide walks you step by step through every core feature. No prior
knowledge required.

## What can Aperio do?

- **Multiple calendars and task lists in one place** – Google, iCloud,
  Outlook/Exchange, Vikunja, Todoist and more, plus local calendars that
  work without any account.
- **Events and tasks** – create, edit, move and repeat them.
- **Contacts** – address books from the same accounts, with labelled phone
  numbers and email addresses, plus birthday calendars derived from them.
- **Different views** – day, week, month, year, agenda and a dedicated task
  view.
- **Reminders** with sounds and snooze.
- **Search** across events and tasks.
- **Cross-device synchronization** via your own storage (e.g. WebDAV or
  Dropbox).
