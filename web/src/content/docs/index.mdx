## What is Aperio?

Aperio shows calendars, tasks and contacts from many sources side by side —
Google, iCloud / CalDAV, Outlook / Microsoft 365, Exchange, Vikunja, Todoist
and purely local lists — in day, week, month, year, agenda and task views.
Accessibility is the project's founding constraint: it is developed and tested
**screen-reader-first**, and every action is reachable from the keyboard.

## Built around accessibility

- Developed and manually tested **screen-reader-first** (primarily NVDA).
- **Keyboard-first:** every action reachable without a mouse; drag-and-drop is
  always a redundant mouse affordance on top of a keyboard path.
- English and German throughout.
